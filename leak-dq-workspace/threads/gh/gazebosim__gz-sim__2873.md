 I did some more digging, removing various parts of the world here and there and that's what I found:

* The effect seems to be cumulative, so both models contribute to the leak but at different rates though. 
* The heightmap model which represents mountains around the island contributes a little bit, while the island itself contributes a lot
  * Island is at about 60 MB/s, heightmap is much smaller, around 0.6MB/s but it is there -- if left to its own devices it will eat up ram
  * Numbers are precise up to the order of magnitude, I just eyeballed them from the Task Manager
* Removing various parts of island model -- like chairs, benches and other stuff -- decreases the mem leak rate
* Removing textures greatly reduces the rate (2.65 MB/s with full world) but that's yeah, not the best solution
