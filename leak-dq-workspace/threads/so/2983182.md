## Question

`new` and `delete` are said to be preprocessors while `malloc` and `free` are functions. What is meant by `new` and `delete` being preprocessors?

## Answer 2983185 [ACCEPTED]

responder · Rhangaun · 2010-06-06T05:10:57Z · score 11 · https://stackoverflow.com/a/2983185

*new* and *delete* are C++ **operators** (like +, (), etc.) whereas *malloc* and *free* are (C) functions. Some operators (including new and delete) can be overloaded.

New and delete are not "preprocessors". They have nothing to do with *the* preprocessor. New and delete are language directives. More precisely, they are unary operators like '+' and '*'.
