## Answer 42711082 [ACCEPTED]

responder · Jay · 2017-03-10T05:42:29Z · score 1 · https://stackoverflow.com/a/42711082

There are 2 problems in your code:

1. You should not be freeing `data` as it is returned from call to `strtok` and is not a malloced pointer and is just a pointer to the string inside of input returned by getline.(This mostly causes your Invalid Free Error in Valgrind Report)

1. You set `input` to NULL at the end of the loop, so you are basically losing the pointer to the memory returned by `getLine`. You should be freeing `input` inside the loop at the appropriate place (This is causing your Memory Leak Error in Valgrind Report)

responder · David C. Rankin · 2017-03-10T05:54:10Z · score 0 · https://stackoverflow.com/questions/42710999/#comment72543446_42711082

If he just happens to have the `data` point to the first token from `strtok`, he would essentially be freeing `input` (which he should do anyway after duplicating and storing `data` in a separate block of memory). Big rule with `getline`, you cannot lose a reference to the beginning of its allocation of `input` or you lose the ability to free the block allocated by `getline`. Always better to allocate new blocks for storing portions of `input` so that `input` can be left for reuse by `getline` (which it does on subsequent reads).
