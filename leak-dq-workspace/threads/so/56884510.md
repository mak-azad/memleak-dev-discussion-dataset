"Is the error related to something in my driver or to the kernel?" - The error message just says that "Unable to handle kernel NULL pointer dereference" occured. Like any other error message, it doesn't contain information whether your driver is **responsible** for the error or not. Assuming the error doesn't hit without loading of your driver, the driver could be perfectly be a reason. We need to see its code (in form of [minimal reproducible example](https://stackoverflow.com/help/minimal-reproducible-example)) for make a conclusion. Without the code your question has a little sense.
