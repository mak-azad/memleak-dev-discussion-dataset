I don't see `munmap*` anywhere in your code, so why is it in the title? I agree with S.P.D. about the "good book" vs "online judge". But, here's a hint: Change: `int l = (int) malloc(sizeof(int));` to `int *l = malloc(sizeof(int));` and `free(&l);` to `free(l);` I believe it's UB ("undefined behavior") to compare pointers that come from two separate `malloc` calls [per ISO/C]. Thus, `if (l > w)` is UB [or minimally nonsensical]. You *can* compare pointers that are within the *same* array/area, but you're *not* doing that. Again, get a good book. Consider Harvard's `cs50` online *course*
